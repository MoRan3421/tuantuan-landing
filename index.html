📜 Server Rules · 服务器规则

Bilingual · 双语版 · Read carefully before participating · 参与前请仔细阅读

1

Respect Everyone · 尊重所有人

Core conduct · 核心行为准则
EN

Treat every member with respect and dignity, regardless of background, language, or opinion. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind are strictly forbidden. Debate ideas — never attack the person.

中

请以尊重和礼貌对待每一位成员，无论其背景、语言或观点如何。严禁骚扰、仇恨言论、歧视或任何形式的人身攻击。可以讨论观点，但绝不攻击个人。

2

No Spam or Flooding · 禁止刷屏

Chat quality · 聊天质量
EN

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, excessive emojis, or meaningless content. No unsolicited advertising, server invites, or self-promotion without prior permission from a Staff member. Keep conversations on-topic per channel.

中

禁止在频道内刷屏、发送大量重复内容或无意义信息。未经管理员许可，禁止发布广告、服务器邀请或自我宣传内容。请在对应频道内保持话题相关性。

3

Safe Content Only · 仅限安全内容

Content policy · 内容政策
EN

Keep all content SFW (Safe for Work) in public channels. NSFW content, graphic violence, gore, or disturbing material is strictly prohibited. No doxxing, real personal information, or private data of any individual.

中

公共频道内请保持内容安全（适合所有年龄）。严禁发布成人内容、暴力血腥或令人不适的图片/视频。禁止人肉搜索、泄露他人真实个人信息或私人数据。

4

Language Use · 语言使用

Communication · 交流规范
EN

Use the correct channel for your language. English in #english-only, Chinese in #chinese-only. General channels support both. Mixing languages is fine in mixed channels — just be understandable.

中

请在对应语言频道内使用相应语言。英文专区请说英文，中文专区请说中文。综合频道支持双语，请保持内容可理解即可。

5

Bot Commands · 机器人指令

Bot usage · 机器人使用
EN

Use bot commands only in #bot-commands unless specified otherwise. Do not spam bot commands. Attempting to exploit or abuse the bot will result in immediate suspension from bot access.

中

机器人指令请仅在 #机器人指令 频道使用（除非特别说明）。禁止刷机器人指令。尝试利用或滥用机器人将被立即暂停使用权限。

6

Follow Staff Decisions · 遵守管理员决定

Authority · 权威
EN

Staff decisions are final. Do not argue publicly with moderation actions — use a support ticket or DM a mod instead. Impersonating staff, evading punishment with alt accounts, or undermining server authority is a permanent ban offence.

中

管理员的决定具有最终效力。请勿在公开频道与管理行为争论——如有异议请开工单或私信管理员。冒充管理员、用小号逃避处罚或破坏服务器秩序，将被永久封禁。

7

Discord ToS · Discord 服务条款

Platform rules · 平台规则
EN

All members must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. This includes age requirements (13+), no account sharing, and no illegal activities.

中

所有成员必须遵守 Discord 服务条款及社区准则，包括年龄要求（13岁以上）、禁止共用账号以及禁止任何违法活动。

⚖️ Consequences · 处罚阶梯

Enforcement is proportional to severity · 处罚与违规严重程度相符

⚠️

轻微违规，初犯

重复或中度违规

✨ About 团团 · 关于团团

Honest version status and roadmap · 真实版本状态与路线图

EN: 团团 is a pre-alpha Discord community bot with a commercial-grade architecture. It runs on TypeScript, Discord.js v14, PostgreSQL, Redis, and BullMQ. The core infrastructure, XP system, economy, daily rewards, moderation, and mini-games are live. More features ship as development continues.

中：团团 是一个预发布版本的 Discord 社区机器人，采用商业级架构设计。基于 TypeScript、Discord.js v14、PostgreSQL、Redis 和 BullMQ 构建。核心基础设施、XP 系统、经济系统、每日签到、管理工具及迷你游戏均已上线，更多功能持续开发中。

Roadmap · 开发路线

✅ v0.0.1 Foundation · 基础架构

TypeScript strict, XP/levels, economy, daily/streak, dice/trivia/guess games, moderation (warn/timeout/ban), multilingual engine, Prisma schema (40+ models), Docker + CI/CD

🔶 v0.1.0 Quest & Achievement Engine · 任务与成就

Quest progress tracking, achievement auto-unlock, title equip, shop (/shop command), inventory system

📋 v0.2.0 Events & Observer · 活动与观测者系统

Seasonal festivals, world boss events, easter egg discovery engine, mysterious Observer progression

📋 v0.3.0 Premium & Web Dashboard · 高级版 & 控制台

Premium tiers, cosmetic shop, owner web console, advanced analytics, birthday system
